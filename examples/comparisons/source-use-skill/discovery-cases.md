# Discovery cohort

Use these four cases to debug the Skill, task wording, and deterministic scorer
before freezing or running the eight holdouts:

1. Travel reimbursement: effective policy versus an older handbook and a newer draft.
2. Support retention: signed DPA versus a marketing FAQ and an unsigned amendment.
3. On-call response: active incident procedure versus a retired runbook and tabletop notes.
4. Contractor access: current security standard versus a superseded checklist and planned control.

Discovery outputs may change the implementation or evaluator. They may not be
added to the holdout after their outcomes are observed.
