Semantics

Keep evidence lanes separate.

Infrastructure, deterministic success, judgment, and mechanism evidence do not collapse into one score.

Absence

Missing remains missing.

Reconciliation

Link every public result.

Claims publish only after terminal coordinates reconcile to authoritative evidence.
