## Question

What authority boundaries turn observations into reversible engineering?

## Intended takeaway

A learning loop requires immutable proposals, external evaluation, and reversible decisions.

## What the film does not claim

More telemetry alone does not create an evaluator, approval authority, or Serverless observation.
